Voters Guideline

Read this before you vote.

  1. Pre-register first. Use the pre-registration form while voting is locked.
  2. Log in to vote. Voting is only available to logged-in voters.
  3. Review candidates. Open View Details to see credentials, achievements, video and photos.
  4. Submit once. Your ballot is final after you submit it.
  5. Keep it private. Don’t share your login with anyone.
